## 摘要<small lang="en">Abstract</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p148-01" data-source-page="148" id="p148-01">

交互式三维计算机图形应用生成的动画，通常从某个角色的主观视点，或者从少量经过策略性布置的视点来呈现。由于忽视了摄影机布置，这些应用未能发挥摄影师多年来一直在探索的重要叙事能力。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 148 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Animations generated by interactive 3D computer graphics applications are typically portrayed either from a particular character&#x27;s point of view or from a small set of strategically-placed viewpoints. By ignoring camera placement, such applications fail to realize important storytelling capabilities that have been explored by cinematographers for many years.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p148-02" data-source-page="148" id="p148-02">

本文介绍电影摄影的若干原则，并说明如何将它们形式化为一种称为声明式摄影机控制语言（Declarative Camera Control Language，DCCL）的声明式语言。我们以一个简单的交互式电子游戏为背景，介绍 DCCL 的应用，并通过编码一本电影教材中的 16 种摄影惯用式，论证 DCCL 可以在与专业导演相同的抽象层次上表达摄影知识。这些惯用式能够产生引人入胜的动画，随文录像带对此进行了展示。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 148 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>In this paper, we describe several of the principles of cinematography and show how they can be formalized into a declarative language, called the Declarative Camera Control Language (DCCL). We describe the application of DCCL within the context of a simple interactive video game and argue that DCCL represents cinematic knowledge at the same level of abstraction as expert directors by encoding 16 idioms from a film textbook. These idioms produce compelling animations, as demonstrated on the accompanying videotape.</p>

</div>
</details>

## 引言<small lang="en">Introduction</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p148-03" data-source-page="148" id="p148-03">

电影语言是一种复杂的语言，自本世纪初以来，经由才华横溢的电影制作者不断努力，逐步发展而来。因此，电影的规则如今已如此常见，以至于观众几乎总是将它们视为理所当然；然而，这些规则有多么不易察觉，就有多么不可或缺。大多数交互式三维计算机图形应用，例如虚拟聊天管理程序、交互式小说环境和电子游戏，都没有利用已经确立的摄影技法。尤其是，大多数计算机动画都从某个角色的主观视点，或者从少量经过策略性布置的视点来呈现。限制摄影机布置，使这些应用未能发挥摄影师数十年来发展出的叙事表达能力。遗憾的是，尽管已有多本教材以非形式化的方式描述了拍摄各种场景的诸多规则（Arijon 1976；Lukas 1985；Mascelli 1965），要把这些教材知识编码得足够精确，让计算机程序能够操作它们，仍然十分困难。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 148 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The language of film is a complex one, which has evolved gradually through the efforts of talented filmmakers since the beginning of the century. As a result the rules of film are now so common that they are nearly always taken for granted by audiences; nonetheless, they are every bit as essential as they are invisible. Most interactive 3D computer graphics applications (e.g., virtual chat managers, interactive fiction environments, and videogames) do not exploit established cinematographic techniques. In particular, most computer animations are portrayed either from a particular character&#x27;s point of view or from a small set of strategically-placed viewpoints. By restricting camera placement, such applications fail to realize the expository capabilities developed by cinematographers over many decades. Unfortunately, while there are several textbooks that contain informal descriptions of numerous rules for filming various types of scenes (Arijon 1976; Lukas 1985; Mascelli 1965), it is difficult to encode this textbook knowledge in a manner that is precise enough for a computer program to manipulate.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p148-04" data-source-page="148" id="p148-04">

本文介绍电影制作的若干原则，说明如何将它们形式化为一种声明式语言，再把这种语言用于交互式电子游戏中的摄影机控制问题。具体来说，我们描述声明式摄影机控制语言 DCCL，并证明它足以编码电影教材中的许多启发式规则。我们还提出摄影机规划系统（Camera Planning System，CPS）：它接收动画轨迹作为输入，返回完整的摄影机规格。CPS 包含一个与应用领域无关的编译器，用于求解 DCCL 约束并计算摄影机的动态控制；还包含一个与应用领域无关的启发式评估器，对编译器生成的候选镜头规格进行质量排序。我们演示了一个简单的交互式电子游戏：它根据用户输入生成仿真动画，再把这些动画送入 CPS，以产生完整的摄影机规格，如随文录像带所示。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 148 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>In this paper, we describe several of the principles of filmmaking, show how they can be formalized into a declarative language, and then apply this language to the problem of camera control in an interactive video game. Specifically, we describe the Declarative Camera Control Language (DCCL) and demonstrate that it is sufficient for encoding many of the heuristics found in a film textbook. We also present a Camera Planning System (CPS), which accepts animation traces as input and returns complete camera specifications. The CPS contains a domain-independent compiler that solves DCCL constraints and calculates the dynamical control of the camera, as well as a domain-independent heuristic evaluator that ranks the quality of the candidate shot specifications that the compiler produces. We demonstrate a simple interactive video game that creates simulated animations in response to user input and then feeds these animations to CPS in order to produce complete camera specifications as shown on the accompanying videotape.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p148-05" data-source-page="148" id="p148-05">

我们的游戏原型为 DCCL 和 CPS 的应用提供了试验平台。不过，DCCL 和 CPS 也可能用于其他多种应用。在电子游戏、多用户地下城（Multi-user Dungeons，MUD）和交互式小说中，自动摄影可以让应用传达某个特定角色的主观印象，而不必诉诸主观视点镜头。<sup><a id="fnref-1" href="#fn-1">1</a></sup> 由于许多 MUD 会长时间持续运行，自动摄影系统可以为用户定制事件摘要，呈现他们离开期间错过的事情。另一方面，自动摄影可以用于与可能参与下一代用户界面的“智能体”建立自然的交互。自动摄影也可以帮助没有专业经验的用户制作桌面视频，或创建动画演示。在后一种情况下，Karp 和 Feiner 已经表明（Karp & Feiner 1990；1993），可以在计算机上有效设计动画演示，从而减少代价高昂的人工参与，并使演示能够针对特定观众或情境进行定制。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 148 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Our prototype video game serves as a testbed for applications of DCCL and CPS. However, there are number of alternative applications to which both DCCL and CPS might be applied. Within the realm of video games, Multi-user Dungeons (MUDs), and interactive fiction, automated cinematography would allow an application to convey the subjective impression of a particular character without resorting to point-of-view shots.¹ Because many MUDs operate over long periods of time, an automated cinematography system could provide users with customized summaries of events they had missed while they were away. Alternatively, automated cinematography could be used to create natural interactions with the “intelligent agents” that are likely to take part in the next generation of user interfaces. Automated cinematography could also be used to assist naive users in the creation of desktop videos, or for building animated presentations. In the latter case, Karp and Feiner have shown (Karp &amp; Feiner 1990; 1993) that animated presentations can be effectively designed on a computer, reducing costly human involvement and allowing presentations to be customized for a particular viewer or situation.</p>

</div>
</details>

## 电影摄影原则<small lang="en">Principles of Cinematography</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p149-01" data-source-page="149" id="p149-01">

虽然可以把一部电影看作仅仅是一串线性排列的画格，但把它理解为具有结构的作品，往往更有帮助。在最高层次上，电影是场景的序列，每个场景捕捉某种特定的情境或动作。电影中的每个场景由一个或多个镜头组成。一个镜头覆盖从摄影机开机到关机之间的那一小段影片。通常，一部电影由大量独立镜头构成，每个镜头的持续时间可以短至一两秒，也可以长至几十秒。<sup><a id="fnref-2" href="#fn-2">2</a></sup>

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 149 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Although a film can be considered to be nothing but a linear sequence of frames, it is often helpful to think of a film as having structure. At the highest level, a film is a sequence of scenes, each of which captures some specific situation or action. Each scene in the film is composed of one or more shots. A single shot covers the small portion of a movie between when a camera is turned on and when it is turned off. Typically, a film is comprised of a large number of individual shots, with each shot&#x27;s duration lasting from a second or two in length to perhaps tens of seconds.²</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

### 摄影机布置<small lang="en">Camera Placement</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p149-02" data-source-page="149" id="p149-02">

导演相对于兴趣轴线（line of interest）来指定摄影机布置。这是一条假想的有向线，可以连接两个互动的演员，也可以沿着演员的运动方向，或沿着演员面朝的方向。图 1 展示了两个相向而立的演员所形成的轴线。

<div lang="en">

<p>Directors specify camera placements relative to the line of interest, an imaginary vector connecting two interacting actors, directed along the line of an actor&#x27;s motion, or oriented in the direction the actor is facing. Figure 1 shows the line formed by two actors facing each other.</p>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p149-03" data-source-page="149" id="p149-03">

从机位 b 拍摄演员 X，称为平行机位（parallel camera placement）；从机位 c 拍摄 X，得到内反打机位（internal reverse placement）；从机位 d 拍摄，得到同时呈现两名演员的顶角镜头（apex shot）；最后，从机位 g 拍摄，称为外反打机位（external reverse placement）。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 149 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Shooting actor X from camera position b is called a parallel camera placement. Filming X from position c yields an internal reverse placement. Shooting from position d results in an apex shot that shows both actors. Finally, filming from g is called an external reverse placement.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p149-04" data-source-page="149" id="p149-04">

摄影师发现，某些“截取高度”可以形成悦目的构图，另一些则会产生难看的结果，例如画面把一个人的脚踝截掉。大致有五种实用的摄影机距离，也就是景别（Arijon 1976，第 18 页）。大特写（extreme closeup）在颈部截取；近景（closeup）在胸部下方或腰部截取；中景（medium view）在胯部或膝部下方截取；全景（full view）显示整个人；远景（long view）则提供远距离的观看视角。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 149 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Cinematographers have identified that certain “cutting heights” make for pleasing compositions while others yield ugly results (e.g., an image of a man cut off at the ankles). There is a set of (roughly) five useful camera distances (Arijon 1976, p. 18). An extreme closeup cuts at the neck; a closeup cuts under the chest or at the waist; a medium view cuts at the crotch or under the knees; a full view shows the entire person; and a long view provides a distant perspective.</p>

</div>
</details>

<div lang="en">

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 149 页</summary>

<p>Parallel editing: Story lines (visualized as scenes) should alternate between different characters, locations, or times.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

**只展示故事的关键时刻：**应当删去叙事中重复的时刻。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 149 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Only show peak moments of the story: Repetitive moments from a narrative should be deleted.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

**不要越轴：**一旦初始镜头从轴线左侧或右侧拍摄，后续镜头就应保持在同一侧；除非用一个中性的交代镜头展示从一侧转到另一侧的过程。这个规则确保：拍摄运动演员的连续镜头，能够保持其画面运动方向一致。

This rule ensures that successive shots of a moving actor will maintain the direction of apparent motion.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

**让演员引领：**所有运动都应由演员先发起，摄影机随后跟上；反过来，在停止时，摄影机应比演员稍早静止。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 149 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Let the actor lead: The actor should initiate all movement, with the camera following; conversely, the camera should come to rest a little before the actor.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p149-10" data-source-page="149" id="p149-10">

**分解运动：**表现运动的场景，应拆成至少两个镜头。通常，每个镜头都应在演员看起来移动了半个画面区域时切换。切换时还应改变摄影机与主体之间的距离。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 149 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Break movement: A scene illustrating motion should be broken into at least two shots. Typically, each shot is cut so that the actor appears to move across half the screen area. A change of the camera-to-subject distance should also be made in the switch.</p>

</div>
</details>

### 摄影惯用式<small lang="en">Idioms</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p149-11" data-source-page="149" id="p149-11">

摄影师最重要的发明，或许就是摄影惯用式（idiom）这个概念：用一组镜头来捕捉某种特定动作的典型方式。例如，在两人对话中，电影制作者可以先用一个顶角视角同时呈现两名演员，再交替展示每个人，有时使用内反打机位，有时使用外反打机位。尽管惯用式的变化无穷无尽，电影导演已学会依赖其中一个较小的子集。实际上，电影书籍（例如 Arijon 1976）主要就是摄影惯用式的汇编，并讨论在何种情境下应优先选择某种惯用式而非另一种。图 2 展示了一种由三个镜头组成的惯用式，本文后续将持续以它为例。这一惯用式改编自 Arijon 教材（1976）的图 13.2，用于表现一名演员短距离接近另一名演员的运动。第一个镜头是近景：演员 X 起初位于画面中央，然后从左侧出画。第二个镜头以演员 Y 的远景开始；演员 X 从右侧画外入画，在 X 到达画面中央时结束。最后一个镜头以 Y 的中景开始，演员 X 从右侧画外入画，停在中央。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 149 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Perhaps the most significant invention of cinematographers is the notion of an idiom — a stereotypical way to capture some specific action as a series of shots. For example, in a dialogue between two people, a filmmaker might begin with an apex view of both actors, and then alternate views of each, at times using internal reverse placements and at times using external reverse. While there is an infinite variety of idioms, film directors have learned to rely on a small subset of these. Indeed, film books (e.g., (Arijon 1976)) are primarily a compilation of idioms along with a discussion of the situations when a filmmaker should prefer one idiom over another. Figure 2 presents a three-shot idiom that serves as an extended example throughout the remainder of this paper. The idiom, adapted from Figure 13.2 of Arijon&#x27;s text (1976), provides a method for depicting short-range motion of one actor approaching another. The first shot is a closeup; actor X begins in the center of the screen and exits left. The second shot begins with a long view of actor Y; actor X enters from off-screen right, and the shot ends when X reaches the center. The final shot begins with a medium view of Y, with actor X entering from off-screen right and stopping at center.</p>

</div>
</details>

## DCCL<small lang="en">Declarative Camera Control Language</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p149-12" data-source-page="149" id="p149-12">

本节对声明式摄影机控制语言 DCCL 作非形式化的介绍。DCCL 规格之所以重要，是因为它使 CPS 能够形式化、编码并实现常见的电影惯用式，例如图 2 所示的惯用式。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 149 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>This section provides an informal description of the Declarative Camera Control Language DCCL. The specification of DCCL is important because it allows CPS to formalize, encode, and implement common film idioms, such as the one presented in Figure 2.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p150-01" data-source-page="150" id="p150-01">

DCCL 有四个基本概念：片段（fragments）、景别（views）、机位布置（placements）和运动端点（movement endpoints）。这些基本元素组合起来，用于指定镜头和摄影惯用式等更高层次的结构。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 150 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>There are four basic primitive concepts in DCCL: fragments, views, placements, and movement endpoints; these primitives are combined to specify higher-level constructs such as shots and idioms.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

### 片段<small lang="en">Fragments</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p150-02" data-source-page="150" id="p150-02">

上一节讨论了摄影师如何将镜头视为电影的基本构建单元。对于我们的自动化系统，我们发现，把每个镜头进一步分解成一个或多个片段的集合很有用。片段指定一个时间区间，在此期间，摄影机的位置和朝向保持不变，或者执行一种简单运动。DCCL 定义了五种片段类型，图 4 对它们作了示意。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 150 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>In the previous section, we discussed how cinematographers treat a shot as the primitive building block in a film. For our automated system, we have found it useful to further decompose each shot into a collection of one or more fragments. A fragment specifies an interval of time during which the camera is in a static position and orientation or is performing a single simple motion. DCCL defines five fragment types (illustrated schematically in Figure 4).</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p150-03" data-source-page="150" id="p150-03">

要完整地指定一个片段，还需要额外信息。有些参数是显而易见的：例如，要跟随演员运动，就必须指定跟随哪名演员，以及在哪个时间区间进行拍摄。此外，还必须指定所需景别——大特写、近景、中景、全景或远景——以及摄影机相对于一个或多个演员的机位布置——内反打、外反打、平行或顶角。其中三种片段，即经过式（go-by）、摇摄（panning）和跟移（tracking），还需要一个称为基准时刻（primary moment）的参数，用于指定：在包含运动的镜头中，机位布置命令应在哪个时刻成立。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 150 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>A fully-specified fragment requires additional information. Some of these arguments are obvious — for example, to track the motion of an actor, one must specify which actor to track and over what time interval to roll the film. In addition, one must also specify the desired camera range — extreme, closeup, medium, full, or long, as well as the placement of the camera relative to the actor (or actors) — internal, external, parallel, and apex. Three of the fragments, go-by, panning, and tracking, require an additional argument, called the primary moment, which specifies the moment at which the placement command is to take effect during a shot in which there is motion.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p150-04" data-source-page="150" id="p150-04">

最后，经过式与摇摄这两种片段还需要另一个参数，称为运动端点，用于表示演员相对于画面应当走过的运动范围。<sup><a id="fnref-3" href="#fn-3">3</a></sup> 如图 5 所示，DCCL 支持七个运动端点关键字。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 150 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Finally, two of these fragments, go-by and panning, require another argument called a movement endpoint, which is used to indicate the range of motion to be covered by the actor relative to the screen.³ As Figure 5 illustrates, DCCL recognizes seven movement-endpoint keywords.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p150-05" data-source-page="150" id="p150-05">

请注意，运动端点关键字虽然指向画面上的位置，却被用于计算经过式与摇摄片段的持续时间。<sup><a id="fnref-4" href="#fn-4">4</a></sup> 例如，图 2 惯用式的第一个镜头，可以定义为从 `center` 运动到 `out-left` 的经过式片段。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 150 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Note that although the movement-endpoint keywords refer to locations on the screen, they are used to calculate the temporal duration of go-by and panning fragments.⁴ For example, the first shot of the idiom of Figure 2 can be defined as a go-by moving from center to out-left.</p>

</div>
</details>

### 镜头与摄影惯用式<small lang="en">Shots and Idioms</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p150-06" data-source-page="150–151" id="p150-06">

在很多情况下，一个镜头由一个片段构成；但有时，将多个片段合并成更复杂的镜头会更有效。例如，可以先用一个摇摄片段，让奔跑的演员从 `out-left` 移动到画面中央；接着停止摄影机旋转，并增加其横向运动，使其与演员速度一致，从而转为一个跟移镜头（图 6）。多片段镜头通常以不同顺序组合摇摄、跟移和经过式片段。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 150–151 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>In many cases, a shot is composed of a single fragment, but sometimes it is effective to merge several fragments together to form a more complex shot. For example, one can start with a panning fragment in which a running actor moves from out-left into the center of the screen, then shift to a tracking shot by terminating camera rotation and increasing its lateral motion to match that of the actor (Figure 6). Multifragment shots typically combine panning, tracking and go-by fragments in different orders.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<details class="psl-english"><summary>英文图注</summary><p lang="en">Figure 6: Schematic illustration of a shot composed of two fragments: a panning fragment that melds imperceptibly into a tracking fragment.</p></details>
</figcaption>
</figure>

<p class="psl-translator-note">图内文字译注：Panning＝摇摄；Tracking＝跟移。</p>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p151-01" data-source-page="151" id="p151-01">

在多片段镜头中，能够让一个片段的结束与下一个片段的开始在“仿真时间”上同步，往往十分重要。因此，DCCL 支持导出计算得到的变量，例如片段的开始或结束时间，供同一惯用式中的其他片段使用。如果片段没有覆盖整个场景，场景的时长就可能缩短，从而产生“时间压缩”。

<details class="psl-english">

<div lang="en">

<p>In multifragment shots, it is often important to be able to synchronize (in “simulation time”) the end of one fragment with the beginning of the next. For this reason, DCCL supports the ability to export computed variables, such as the starting or ending time of a fragment, for use by other fragments in the same idiom. The duration of a scene can decrease if fragments do not cover the entire scene, producing “time contraction.”</p>

</div>
</details>

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 151 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

For example, Figure 3 shows the actual DCCL encoding of the idiom illustrated in Figure 2; this idiom is a good choice for showing one actor approaching another.</p>

## 摄影机规划系统<small lang="en">The Camera Planning System</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p151-03" data-source-page="151" id="p151-03">

摄影机规划系统（CPS）将前文介绍的电影摄影机布置原则编码并实现，用于一名或两名演员的简单运动序列。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 151 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The Camera Planning System (CPS) codifies and implements the previously described cinematographic principles of camera placement for the case of simple movement sequences on the part of one or two actors.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p151-04" data-source-page="151" id="p151-04">

CPS 的输入是动画轨迹：每个角色的位置与活动信息，以及指定在哪些时间区间拍摄哪些演员的指令。在我们的交互式游戏中，这些信息由一个简单的计算机仿真产生，而仿真则响应用户命令生成。有了这些轨迹信息，CPS 会选择要用摄影机捕捉哪些时间子区间，以及采用哪些视点和镜头焦段，也就是采用多大的视场角。随后，动画就可以按照 CPS 选定的时间区间与摄影机布置回放给用户。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 151 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>As input, CPS requires an animation trace: information about the positions and activities of each character, as well as directives stating which actors are to be filmed over what intervals of time. In our interactive game, this information is produced by a simple computer simulation generated in response to a user command. Given this trace information, the CPS chooses which subintervals of time to capture with the camera and from which vantage points and with which lenses (i.e., what field of view). The animation can then be played back to the user using the intervals and camera placements selected by the CPS.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p151-05" data-source-page="151" id="p151-05">

CPS 使用的主要数据结构是影片树（film tree，图 7），它表示正在生成的影片。树中的场景层与候选惯用式层尤为重要：影片中的每个场景都关联一个或多个可能的惯用式，每个惯用式代表拍摄其父级场景的一种特定方式。CPS 会不断展开影片树，直到为每个惯用式编译出详细解，再为每个场景选择合适的候选惯用式与画格。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 151 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The primary data structure used by CPS is the film tree (Figure 7), which represents the film being generated. Of primary consequence are the scene and candidate idiom levels of the tree: each scene in the film is associated with one or more possible idioms, with each idiom representing a particular method of filming the parent scene. The CPS operates by expanding the film tree until it has compiled detailed solutions for each idiom, and then selecting appropriate candidate idioms and frames for each scene.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

### 序列规划器<small lang="en">The Sequence Planner</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p151-07" data-source-page="151–152" id="p151-07">

CPS 序列规划器当前的实现相当简单。与 CPS 的其他部分不同，序列规划器的实现代码针对应用的领域，也就是情节而编写，例如追逐与捕捉交互。规划器接收动画轨迹作为输入，这些轨迹描述一组演员随时间变化的行为；输出则是一个已经指定到场景层的影片树（图 7）。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 151–152 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The current implementation of the CPS sequence planner is quite simple. Unlike the other portions of the CPS the code implementing the sequence planner is specific to the domain (plot) of the application (e.g. chase and capture interactions). As input the planner receives an animation trace describing the time-varying behavior of a group of actors. As output, the sequence planner produces a film tree (Figure 7) that is specified down to the scene level.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p152-01" data-source-page="152" id="p152-01">

动画轨迹为动画中的每一帧指定每名演员的位置、速度与关节位置。轨迹还标注每名演员在每一帧正在进行的活动，并把更高层次的信息编码为一组影片段落。每个影片段落包含一个时间区间、一名用作主角的演员，以及可选的第二名演员。在当前应用中，我们使用多个影片段落产生平行剪辑效果：让 CPS 将表现一组演员的场景，与表现另一组演员的场景交织起来，见随文录像带。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 152 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The animation trace specifies position, velocity, and joint positions for each actor for each frame of the animation. The trace also labels the activity being performed by each actor in each frame, as well as higher-level information encoded as a set of film sequences, with each film sequence including an interval of time, an actor to use as the protagonist, and (optionally) a second actor. In the current application, multiple film sequences are used to create parallel editing effects by having the CPS intermix scenes featuring one set of actors with scenes featuring a different set of actors (see accompanying videotape).</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p152-02" data-source-page="152" id="p152-02">

依据动画轨迹中的信息，序列规划器首先按照主角在给定段落中进行的活动，对每个影片段落进行分区，从而生成场景。针对当前应用，我们识别了十种活动类型（表 1）。完成分区后，序列规划器根据每个分区的活动、演员和时间区间，生成带有这些参数的场景。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 152 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Given the information in the animation trace, the sequence planner generates scenes by first partitioning each film sequence according to the activities performed by the protagonist during the given sequence. For the current application we have identified ten activity types (Table 1). After partitioning a sequence, the sequence planner generates scenes parameterized by the activity, actors, and time interval of each partition.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

The planner instantiates idioms by substituting actual parameters (actor names, and scene start and ending times) for the placeholders specified in the idiom definitions. References to actor placements on the right or left sides will be automatically mirrored later, during the idiom solving process.</p>

### DCCL 编译器<small lang="en">The DCCL Compiler</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p152-04" data-source-page="152" id="p152-04">

DCCL 编译器利用演员的运动信息，把每个候选惯用式中的片段展开为可以直接播放的逐帧规格数组。一帧由摄影机的三维位置、朝向与视场角共同完全确定，因此，编译器只需为每个候选惯用式中每个镜头的每个片段，生成这些数值的数组。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 152 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The DCCL compiler uses information about the movement of the actors to expand the fragments in each candidate idiom into an array of frame specifications, which can be played directly. Since a frame is fully constrained by the combination of its camera&#x27;s 3D position, orientation, and field of view, the compiler need only generate an array of these values for each fragment in each shot in each candidate idiom.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p152-05" data-source-page="152" id="p152-05">

最简单的惯用式仅包含一个镜头，而该镜头仅由一个片段构成，因此先讨论这种情况。如果片段类型是 `pov`，编译过程就很简单，所以我们假设片段类型为 `static`、`tracking`、`go-by` 或 `panning`。我们把编译器的工作分成四项任务：

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 152 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>In its simplest form, an idiom consists of a single shot that is composed of a single fragment, so we cover that case first. If the fragment has type pov, then compilation is trivial, so we assume that the fragment has type static, tracking, go-by, or panning. We decompose the compiler&#x27;s job into four tasks:</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p152-06" data-source-page="152" id="p152-06">

**1.** 确定适当的基准时刻。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 152 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>1. Determine the appropriate primary moment(s).</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p152-07" data-source-page="152" id="p152-07">

**2.** 为每个基准时刻确定可能的逐帧规格集合。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 152 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>2. Determine the set of possible frame specifications for each primary moment.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p152-08" data-source-page="152" id="p152-08">

**3.** 在给定初始帧规格的条件下，计算片段的持续时间，也就是帧数组的长度。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 152 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>3. Calculate the temporal duration (length of the frame array) of the fragment given an initial frame specification.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p152-09" data-source-page="152" id="p152-09">

**4.** 根据第 *n − 1* 帧的规格，生成第 *n* 帧的插值规格。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 152 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>4. Generate the interpolated specification of frame n from that of frame n − 1.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p152-10" data-source-page="152–153" id="p152-10">

完成这些任务后，编译器只需为每个基准时刻和帧规格生成一个帧数组。在当前版本的 CPS 中，通常只有两个帧数组，分别对应把摄影机放在兴趣轴线的一侧或另一侧。选择合适一侧的任务留给启发式评估器。<sup><a id="fnref-5" href="#fn-5">5</a></sup>

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 152–153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Once these tasks have been completed, the compiler simply has to generate a frame array for each primary moment and frame specification. In the current version of CPS there are typically only two frame arrays corresponding to placing the camera on one side of the line of interest or the other. The task of choosing the appropriate side is left up to the heuristic evaluator.⁵</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-01" data-source-page="153" id="p153-01">

片段的基准时刻定义了这样一个时间点：在该时刻，摄影机布置应当满足该片段指定的布置。它随片段类型而变化。经过式、跟移和摇摄片段将基准时刻指定为片段的第一个或最后一个时间刻度。静止片段则不指定基准时刻，所以在当前版本的 CPS 中，我们分别在片段时间区间的起点、终点与中点求解机位布置；启发式评估器稍后会判断哪个解看起来最好，并剪去其余解。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The primary moment of a fragment defines the point in time at which the camera placement should conform to the placement specified for the fragment, and varies with the type of fragment. The go-by, tracking, and panning fragments specify the primary moment as either the first or last tick of the fragment. The static fragments, on the other hand, do not specify a primary moment, so in the current version of CPS we solve the placement for the first, last, and midpoint ticks of the fragment&#x27;s time interval; the heuristic evaluator will later determine which solution looks best and prune the rest.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-02" data-source-page="153" id="p153-02">

惯用式指定的片段机位布置，例如内反打、外反打、平行或顶角，与动画轨迹提供的演员位置相结合，将摄影机的初始位置约束在两个向量之一上，具体取决于使用轴线的哪一侧（图 1）。摄影机在这个向量上的实际位置，由摄影机与主要主体之间的预期距离决定。这个距离又由片段的景别——大特写、近景、中景、全景或远景——以及镜头焦距来指定。编译器尝试使用标准镜头，例如 60 度视场角，生成一组适当的机位布置。这些计算背后的向量代数，见 Blinn（1988）。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The fragment&#x27;s placement (e.g., internal, external, parallel, or apex), as specified in the idiom, combined with the location of the actors (from the animation trace) constrains the camera&#x27;s initial position to lie on one of two vectors, according to the side of the line being used (Figure 1). The actual location on this vector is determined by the desired distance between the camera and the primary subject. This distance, in turn, is specified by the fragment&#x27;s range (extreme, closeup, medium, full, or long) and the lens focal length. The compiler attempts to generate a set of appropriate placements using a normal lens (e.g., a 60-degree field of view). The vector algebra behind these calculations is explained in (Blinn 1988).</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-03" data-source-page="153" id="p153-03">

静止、跟移与主观视点片段的持续时间，作为 DCCL 规格的一部分被显式指定。但是，经过式与摇摄片段的持续时间，必须根据运动端点规格并结合演员速度来计算。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The temporal duration of static, tracking, and pov fragments is specified explicitly as part of the DCCL specification. However, the duration of go-by and panning fragments must be computed from the movement-endpoint specification in conjunction with the actor&#x27;s velocity.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-04" data-source-page="153" id="p153-04">

用来逐帧更新摄影机位置与朝向的函数，取决于相关片段的类型，以及演员位置的变化。静止片段和经过式片段不改变摄影机位置或朝向。跟移片段中的摄影机保持朝向不变，但根据演员的速度向量改变位置。摇摄片段中的摄影机保持位置不变，但改变朝向，其角速度受到演员速度以及演员最接近摄影机时的距离约束；这个最近距离由基准时刻决定。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The function used to update the camera position and orientation from one frame to the next depends on the type of fragment involved and the change in the actors&#x27; positions. Static and go-by fragments do not change camera position or orientation. The camera in tracking fragments maintains its orientation, but changes its position based on the actor&#x27;s velocity vector. The camera in panning fragments maintains its position, but changes its orientation with angular velocity constrained by actor velocity and the distance at the closest approach to the camera (as determined by the primary moment).</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-05" data-source-page="153" id="p153-05">

请注意，与序列规划器不同，DCCL 编译器完全不依赖具体应用领域：编译器只依赖几何信息，而不依赖动画的情节或主题。此外，惯用式数据库中的 DCCL 规格可用于不同动画；例如，我们数据库中的惯用式，应当适用于任何包含双角色互动的动画。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Note that unlike the sequence planner, the DCCL compiler is completely domain-independent in that the compiler depends only on geometry and not on the plot or subject of the animation. Furthermore, the DCCL specifications in the idiom database are applicable across various animations; for example, the idioms in our database should apply to any animation with two-character interactions.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

### 启发式评估器<small lang="en">The Heuristic Evaluator</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-06" data-source-page="153" id="p153-06">

由于影片树是一个与／或图（AND/OR graph），它表示许多可能的影片。<sup><a id="fnref-6" href="#fn-6">6</a></sup> 启发式评估器选择能最好地呈现场景的候选惯用式：先对各个片段的帧数组评分并剪枝，再为每个惯用式分配一个实数评分。请注意，在编译之前，不可能估计一个片段或惯用式的质量，因为视觉质量取决于片段类型与演员运动之间复杂的几何相互作用。当演员处于某种相对位置时，一个惯用式可能表现得非常好；而在另一种配置下，它的效果可能很差。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Since the film tree is an AND/OR graph, it represents many possible films.⁶ The heuristic evaluator chooses the candidate idiom that renders a scene best, assigning each idiom a real-valued score by first scoring and pruning the frame arrays of its fragments. Note that it is not possible to estimate the quality of a fragment or idiom before it is compiled, because visual quality is determined by the complex, geometric interactions between the fragment type and the motions of the actors. A given idiom might work extremely well when the actors stand in one position relative to one another, yet work poorly given a different configuration.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-07" data-source-page="153" id="p153-07">

评分机制主要着眼于片段之间的评价标准，即：

- 保持机位布置之间的平顺过渡；
- 排除会导致摄影机越轴的片段。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The scoring mechanism is primarily focused towards evaluating inter-fragment criteria, namely: maintaining smooth transitions between camera placements; eliminating fragments which cause the camera to cross the line.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-08" data-source-page="153" id="p153-08">

此外，评分机制还处理 CPS 编译阶段有时会产生的某些片段内部行为，例如：

- 惩罚过短或过长的片段；
- 排除摄影机反向摇摄的片段。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>In addition, the scoring mechanism deals with certain intra-fragment behaviors that sometimes arise from the compilation phase of the CPS such as: penalizing very short or very long fragments; eliminating fragments in which the camera pans backwards.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-09" data-source-page="153" id="p153-09">

评估器为将要纳入影片的每个场景选出最佳惯用式后，摄影机规划过程就完成了。CPS 将影片树中所有剩余惯用式节点的帧数组连接起来，把相应的帧序列输出给播放器进行渲染。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>After the evaluator has selected the best idiom for each scene to be included in the film, the camera planning process is complete. CPS concatenates the frame arrays for all idiom nodes remaining in the film tree and outputs the corresponding sequence of frames to the player for rendering.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-10" data-source-page="153" id="p153-10">

请注意，评估器的规则虽然是启发式的，但在电影与动画范围内，它们也不依赖具体应用领域：每条规则都编码了一项广泛适用的知识，用来判断哪些类型的镜头在影片中好看，哪些不好看。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Note that while the evaluator&#x27;s rules are heuristic, they are also domain-independent within the domain of film and animation: each rule encodes broadly-applicable knowledge about which types of shots look good on film, and which do not.</p>

</div>
</details>

## 示例应用<small lang="en">Sample Application</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p153-11" data-source-page="153–154" id="p153-11">

我们尤其关注自动摄影的交互式用途。因此，我们决定制作一个简单的交互式游戏，用 CPS 来拍摄人类玩家下达命令后产生的动作。游戏的基本情节很简单：主角 Bob 必须在一个虚拟世界中四处行走，寻找圣杯；我们的实现使用 SGI 的 Performer Town。游戏中还加入了一只企图偷走圣杯的恶犬 Fido，让过程更有趣。游戏不时生成 Fido 的活动动画，并指示 CPS 将这些动画剪辑到当前行动中。最终，Bob 和 Fido 会发生互动：如果 Fido 拿到了圣杯，Bob 就必须追赶 Fido，把它夺回来。用户通过弹出菜单指挥 Bob。这些命令分为四大类：让 Bob 朝某个方向看、移动到画面上的某个点、拾取物体，或追逐另一名演员。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 153–154 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>We are particularly interested in interactive uses of automatic cinematography. Therefore, we decided to build a simple interactive game that would use CPS to film actions commanded by a human player. The basic plot of the game is very simple. The main character, Bob, must walk around a virtual world (in our case, SGI&#x27;s Performer Town) looking for the Holy Grail. The game is made more interesting by the introduction of Fido, an evil dog that seeks to steal the Grail. From time to time, the game generates animations of Fido&#x27;s activities and instructs CPS to edit these animations into the action. Eventually, Bob and Fido interact: if Fido gets the grail, Bob has to chase Fido in order to get it back. The user commands Bob through a pop-up menu. These commands fall into four basic categories: telling Bob to look in a particular direction, to move to a particular point on the screen, to pick up an object, or to chase another actor.</p>

</div>
</details>

<p>The implementation of the game and its various animation/simulation modules was done in C++ using the IRIS Performer toolkit running on SGI workstations, and based partially on the perfly application provided by SGI (a Performer-based walkthrough application). The game itself (not counting CPS or the code present in the existing perfly application) required approximately 10,000 lines of C++ code. The CPS system is also written in C++ (despite the Lisp-like appearance of DCCL) and implemented with about 19,000 lines of code.</p>

## 相关工作<small lang="en">Related Work</small>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p154-03" data-source-page="154" id="p154-03">

利用电影摄影原则来控制摄影机位置与场景结构，这个主题在计算机图形学和人工智能领域受到的关注相对有限。这里我们概述大部分相关工作。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 154 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>The subject of using principles from cinematography to control camera positions and scene structure has received relatively little attention in the computer graphics or AI communities. We survey most of the related work here.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p154-04" data-source-page="154" id="p154-04">

He、Cohen 和 Salesin（1996）利用 DCCL 背后的部分思想，开发了一个实时控制摄影机布置的系统。他们的工作着重于拍摄多个动画角色之间的对话，并使用有限状态机来选择和生成摄影机位置。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 154 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>He, Cohen, and Salesin (1996) have developed a system for controlling camera placement in real-time using some of the ideas behind DCCL. Their work focuses on filming dialogues between multiple animated characters, and uses a finite state machine to select and generate camera positions.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p154-05" data-source-page="154" id="p154-05">

已有一些系统研究如何在执行给定交互任务时，自动把摄影机放到有利的位置（Gleicher & Witkin 1992；Mackinlay、Card & Robertson 1990；Phillips、Badler & Granieri 1992）。不过，这些系统既没有尝试创建场景序列，也没有在制定其规格时应用电影摄影规则。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 154 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>A number of systems have been described for automatically placing the camera in an advantageous position when performing a given interactive task (Gleicher &amp; Witkin 1992; Mackinlay, Card, &amp; Robertson 1990; Phillips, Badler, &amp; Granieri 1992). However, these systems neither attempt to create sequences of scenes, nor do they apply rules of cinematography in developing their specifications.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p154-06" data-source-page="154" id="p154-06">

与我们的工作更接近的是 Karp 和 Feiner 的研究（Karp & Feiner 1990；1993）：他们描述了一个用于自动生成演示的动画规划系统。他们重点关注规划引擎本身，而本文更关心如何定义一种高层声明式语言，用来编码电影摄影的专业知识。因此，这两种方法是互补的。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 154 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>In work that is closer to our own, Karp and Feiner (Karp &amp; Feiner 1990; 1993) describe an animation planning system for generating automatic presentations. Their emphasis is on the planning engine itself, whereas the work described in this paper is more concerned with the problem of defining a high-level declarative language for encoding cinematic expertise. Thus, the two approaches complement each other.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p154-07" data-source-page="154" id="p154-07">

Strassman（1994）报告了 Divaldo，这是一项雄心勃勃的实验，旨在创建“桌面戏剧”的原型系统。与我们专注于摄影机布置不同，Strassman 试图创建能够响应自然语言命令的半自主演员。CPS 与 Divaldo 同样具有互补性。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 154 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Strassman (Strassman 1994) reports on Divaldo, an ambitious experiment to create a prototype system for “desktop theatre.” Unlike our focus on camera placement, Strassman attempts to create semi-autonomous actors who respond to natural language commands. CPS is also complementary to Divaldo.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p154-08" data-source-page="154" id="p154-08">

Drucker 等人的工作（Drucker、Galyean & Zeltzer 1992；Drucker & Zeltzer 1994；1995）关注的问题是：在约束条件下，为单个镜头设置最优摄影机位置。他们采用通用的连续优化范式，为给定镜头自动调整具体摄影机参数。在我们的工作中，一组可能的摄影机配置由 DCCL 中的镜头描述和场景几何完全指定。从这一组不同镜头中进行最终选择时，依据的是每个镜头对场景的覆盖效果。我们的方法不需要通用优化搜索，而且能够保证结果具有常见的镜头形式；代价是可能的摄影机规格集合被大幅缩小。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 154 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Drucker et al. (Drucker, Galyean, &amp; Zeltzer 1992; Drucker &amp; Zeltzer 1994; 1995) are concerned with the problem of setting up the optimal camera position for individual shots, subject to constraints. Specific camera parameters are automatically tuned for a given shot based on a general-purpose continuous optimization paradigm. In our work, a set of possible cameras is fully specified by the shot descriptions in DCCL and the geometry of the scene. The final selection from among this set of different shots is made according to how well each shot covers the scene. Our approach avoids the need for generic optimization searches, and it is guaranteed to result in a common shot form. The cost is a greatly reduced set of possible camera specifications.</p>

</div>
</details>

</div>

<div class="psl-translation-unit" data-source-block="p154-09" data-source-page="154" id="p154-09">

另有几本实用著作介绍了如何根据场景几何推导底层摄影机参数（Foley 等 1990；Hearn & Baker 1994；Blinn 1988）。

<details class="psl-english">
<summary>英文原文 · 第 154 页</summary>
<div lang="en">

<p>Several useful texts derive low-level camera parameters, given the geometry of the scene (Foley et al. 1990; Hearn &amp; Baker 1994; Blinn 1988).</p>

</div>
</details>
